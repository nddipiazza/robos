**Checkout** is a four-step wizard: **Pick a car, Invoice, Payment, Hand back**.

## Step 1: Pick the car

You see every car that is finished and ready to be billed. Cars still being worked on are listed separately, so you can tell a customer how much longer to wait.

::shot[only-finished-cars-are-ready-for-checkout|2|Only finished cars are ready for checkout]

Finished tow calls are listed below the cars, under **Tow calls to bill**. They have their own four steps; see [Run a tow service](../10-tow-desk/).

## Step 2: Invoice

The invoice shows the approved estimate, the hours worked and the cost of the parts. The total comes from the estimate the customer approved. Press **Issue invoice**.

::shot[issue-the-invoice-from-the-approved-estimate|7|The invoice step shows what the customer approved]

If the job needs a small extra charge, enter it before you issue the invoice.

> **Safety net.** An extra charge of more than 10% over the approved estimate is refused. Ask the customer to approve a new estimate first.

::shot[charges-well-above-the-estimate-need-the-customer-s-approval|8|A charge well above the estimate is refused]

## Step 3: Take payment

Enter the amount, choose **Card**, **Cash**, **Check** or **Bank transfer**, and press **Record payment**. The amount is filled in with the balance. If the customer pays in two goes, record each one. The balance drops every time.

::shot[take-payment-in-more-than-one-go|9|After a part payment, the balance is updated]

> **Important.** This records a payment you have already received. Auto Center does not charge cards. Take the card payment on your terminal first, then record it here.

The wizard will not let you move on while anything is still owed.

::shot[the-customer-cannot-leave-with-a-balance|10|The wizard blocks hand-back while a balance remains]

## Step 4: Hand back the keys

Enter who collected the car and press **Hand over keys and close ticket**. The ticket is closed, the car leaves the shop floor, and the payment is in your reports.

::shot[pay-in-full-hand-over-the-keys-and-close-the-ticket|12|A closed ticket]

## You are ready when

- [ ] You have checked out a test car from invoice to keys
- [ ] You know where to record a part payment
