EARNINGS & EXPENSES

Operating profit

Earned revenue, actual costs, and the margin left over.

How profit is calculated

Earned sales minus actual expenses on their effective dates, whether paid or unpaid. Record sales when earned, excluding sales tax; record parts used, actual labor cost (not customer labor charges), supplies and overhead when incurred. Deposits, payment refunds and scanned invoices do not automatically change profit. Record sales credits separately when a refund reduces earned revenue. Inventory purchases become job costs when consumed; avoid counting them twice. Costs of open jobs are included on the date incurred. Totals reflect entered records only, before income tax, financing and owner distributions.
