2. Native Linux PipeWire Pro-Audio & 20-Channel Scarlett 18i20 + Behringer ADA8200 ADAT Capture

Our live studio rack pairs a Focusrite Scarlett 18i20 (3rd Gen) (8 Scarlett analog mic/line preamps, Ch 1–8) with a Behringer Ultragain Digital ADA8200 (8 MIDAS mic preamplifiers over ADAT Optical Toslink, Ch 9–16) to capture 12+ drum microphones simultaneously. On Linux, PipeWire's default UCM profile splits an 18-input USB interface into separate virtual nodes (Analog 1–8 vs Direct2 ADAT 9–16); spawning independent capture processes per node introduces inter-process start skew and phase comb-filtering across close mics and overheads. RobOS DAW Studio (robos-lib/audio-devices.js, lib/daw-engine.js) solves this directly at the kernel hardware bus:

  • Zero-Drift Unified 20-Channel Clock (hw:USB,0): Unifies Focusrite Analog UCM split sources and discrete 8-channel ADAT Optical expansion (Ch 9..Ch 16) onto a single sample-locked 20-channel ALSA capture stream with sub-sample phase coherence across overheads, close mics, and room mics.
  • Real-Time 20 ms Waveform Streaming & 5-Decimal Quiet Transient Precision: Armed tracks stream live peaks every 20 ms while recording, preserving 5-decimal linear precision down to -68 dBFS so quiet ghost notes and un-boosted dynamic mics visibly register on the timeline while preamp silence stays 1-pixel flat.
  • Sub-Millisecond Overdub Headphone Latency Compensation: Measures PipeWire + WebAudio output presentation timestamps so overdubbed drum or guitar passes align automatically to backing tracks without manual nudging.
  • DrumSet AI Soundcheck & 18-Profile Add Mic Wizard: Arm all Focusrite + ADAT inputs at once, play a short check around the kit, and let Gemini classify each channel's spectral/transient signature — or add individual mics with tap-to-detect channel discovery.

3. Real-Time Console DSP, Articulate Metal Kick Mid-Punch & Sound Replacement

Un-triggered acoustic bass drum mics recorded with a felt beater naturally concentrate 20–26 dB more energy at 60–110 Hz than in the 900 Hz – 2.2 kHz midrange where the human ear distinguishes rapid 16th-note double-kick strokes. Boosting a low shelf only swamps the VCA compressor and turns fast patterns into low rumble.

4. Full-Width Workspace, Skinny Telemetry Bar & Multi-Format Range Mixdown

Every pixel of horizontal and vertical space is dedicated to your tracks: navigation moved into the topbar (Audio I/O, Keymap, VST & Samples, Rescan), transport startup locks #btn-transport-play synchronously during WAV decoding so rapid Space presses can never spawn duplicate audio passes, and a skinny 24px bottom grey status toolbar keeps live session metrics visible without ever shifting the timeline:

  • Click-Drag & Right-Click Timeline Selection: Drag across the timeline ruler to highlight any From -> To range (with draggable left/right edge handles and double-click to clear), or right-click anywhere on the ruler/lanes and choose Set Selection 'From' Here / Set Selection 'To' Here.
  • Skinny 24px Bottom Grey Telemetry Toolbar: Segmented panels display SAVED / MODIFIED + single-line status logs, live SELECTION LENGTH (duration, timestamps, and bar count), TRACK LENGTH for the focused channel, SESSION LENGTH (total time & bars at current BPM), TRACKS (channels, armed inputs, takes), and ENGINE (sample rate & PipeWire block latency).
  • 7 Studio Mixdown Formats via FFmpeg: Export stereo masters to MP3 (320k / 256k / 192k CBR or VBR V0), WAV (24-bit Studio PCM, 32-bit Float, 16-bit CD), FLAC (24-bit lossless), M4A / AAC, OGG Vorbis, Opus, and AIFF at 44.1, 48, or 96 kHz.
