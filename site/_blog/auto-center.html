Backed by public vehicle Knowledge Graph schemas, RobOS Auto Center (packages/auto-center) serves both individual vehicle owners (repair history backups, invoice OCR, bulk safety recall checks) and independent repair shops across four dedicated workspaces:

  • Shop Admin & Catalog Import: Configure shop details, default hourly labor rates, team roles, supplier parts price lists, and validated bulk customer/vehicle CSV imports.
  • Front Desk Intake & Estimates: Look up returning customers by phone, walk through structured multi-choice service intake questions, type-ahead vehicle makes/models/years/OBD-II trouble codes, and record customer estimate approvals.
  • Mechanic Bench & Checkout Guards: Mechanics claim tickets, time labor with a built-in stopwatch, log diagnostic findings, and order/receive parts (blocking job completion while parts remain on order). Checkout enforces customer re-approval if charges exceed the estimate and prevents releasing keys with an unpaid balance.
