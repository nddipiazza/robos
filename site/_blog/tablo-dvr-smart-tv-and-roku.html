What We Built & Why

  • Zero-Cloud HMAC-MD5 Tablo 4th Gen Client (packages/robos-lib/tablo-client.js): Tablo 4th Gen DVRs (model TF1282B-01, firmware 2.2.54) expose an HTTP API on port 8887 that rejects unsigned requests. tablo-client.js implements the canonical 4-line HMAC-MD5 request signature (METHOD\nPATH\nMD5(BODY)\nDATE), discovers Tablo units on the LAN, batch-fetches all recording categories (/recordings/sports/events, /recordings/series/episodes, /recordings/movies/airings) and OTA guide channels (/guide/channels), and requests fresh playback tokens via POST /recordings/.../watch.
  • Broadcast Recording Thumbnail Acquisition + Live Frame Capture for Active Recordings: Completed recordings expose a 16:9 broadcast snapshot (video_details.snapshot_image.image_id) on the Tablo, which RobOS downloads and caches in ~/.cache/robos/tablo-images/ and bundles as pkg:/images/tablo-<id>.jpg inside the Roku SceneGraph channel. For in-progress recordings (state: "recording", where Tablo sets snapshot_image: null until the broadcast ends), fetchTabloImage() automatically opens the recording's live HLS stream and captures a 640×360 JPEG frame via ffmpeg -frames:v 1 so active sports and news recordings immediately display a real broadcast thumbnail on both desktop and the 65" Roku TV.
  • Desktop H.264/AAC Live Transcoding & Electron CSP Fix (packages/robos-lib/tv-engine.js, packages/robos-tv/renderer/index.html): Over-the-air ATSC broadcasts use MPEG-2 video and Dolby AC-3 audio, which Chromium's HTML5 <video> element cannot decode natively. The portal server's GET /api/tablo/stream endpoint spawns an ultra-low-latency ffmpeg pipeline (-c:v libx264 -preset ultrafast -tune zerolatency -c:a aac -f mp4 -movflags frag_keyframe+empty_moov+default_base_moof) with seek offset support (?startSec=), and we fixed the renderer's Content-Security-Policy (replacing the invalid http://192.168.* wildcard with standard http: https: scheme sources) so local playback starts immediately.
  • 65" Roku OS SceneGraph TABLO DVR Tab & Native 60fps HLS Rewriter (/api/tablo/roku.m3u8): We expanded Row 1 of the Roku SceneGraph channel (MainScene.xml / MainScene.brs) and the :19210/movie-player portal to 3 top-level image tabs (MOVIES, TV SHOWS, TABLO DVR), added a dedicated 16:9 broadcast thumbnail frame in the Hero Spotlight banner, and built GET /api/tablo/roku.m3u8 to dynamically fetch a fresh Tablo watch lease and rewrite relative /stream/seg.ts and /stream/pl_iframe.m3u8 URIs to absolute LAN URLs on 192.168.1.55:80—allowing the Roku TV's hardware decoder to play 60fps OTA broadcasts directly with zero transcoding.
