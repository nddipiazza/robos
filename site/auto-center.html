RobOS Auto · Maintenance center

Run a repair shop from first call to paid and returned

Auto Center follows every customer car through intake, diagnosis, estimate, approval, repair, invoice, payment and offboarding. Each repair is a numbered ticket, and every step is backed up to storage such as GitHub so you never lose your shop's records. This guide is built from the same end-to-end tests that prove it works: 45 scenarios, 45 passing, every screenshot taken from the real app.

Step 1

Build your shop team

Before a car comes in, set up the people who will work on it. Teammates can be linked to a person in RobOS People so the shop uses the same identities as the rest of RobOS, and each teammate's hours are tracked per repair.

Add a technician linked to a RobOS person

Open Repair lifecycle, expand Add a teammate, enter a name and role, and pick the matching person from RobOS People. The link keeps the shop roster in step with your RobOS directory. Hourly labor cost is optional and feeds the profit reports.

How this is tested (2 steps, passing)
  1. When I add the teammate "Maria Lopez" as "Master technician" linked to the RobOS person "Maria Lopez"
  2. Then the team roster lists "Maria Lopez · Master technician · RobOS: Maria Lopez"

Add a teammate who is not in RobOS yet

Not everyone has a RobOS account. Leave the person set to "Not linked" and the teammate still works on repairs; you can link them later.

How this is tested (2 steps, passing)
  1. When I add the teammate "Chris Park" as "Apprentice"
  2. Then the team roster lists "Chris Park · Apprentice"

Guard rail The same teammate cannot be added twice

The roster refuses a second teammate with the same name or the same RobOS person, so hours are never split across duplicate records.

How this is tested (4 steps, passing)
  1. Given the shop team has "Maria Lopez" as "Master technician" linked to the RobOS person "Maria Lopez"
  2. When I add the teammate "Maria Lopez" as "Technician"
  3. Then I am told "This teammate already exists"
  4. And the team roster does not list "Maria Lopez" twice

Guard rail A malformed hourly cost is rejected

Labor cost must be an amount such as 30.00. Anything else is refused with a plain message.

How this is tested (2 steps, passing)
  1. When I add the teammate "Dan Okafor" as "Technician" with an hourly cost of "thirty"
  2. Then I am told "Enter an amount with up to two decimals"

Step 2

Add a customer

Customers are the people who bring cars in. A customer can be linked to a RobOS user and to a RobOS group such as a fleet account, so permissions and notifications follow the same directory as the rest of RobOS.

Add a customer linked to a RobOS user and group

Expand Add a customer, enter their contact details, then choose the matching RobOS user and, for fleet or business accounts, a RobOS group.

  2. Then the customer list shows "Priya Shah · RobOS user: Priya Shah · group: Fleet customers · 0 vehicle(s)"

Add a walk-in customer with only contact details

A walk-in does not need a RobOS account. A name is enough; phone and email help you reach them about the estimate.

Step 3

Register the customer's vehicle

Each vehicle belongs to a customer and keeps its own repair history, so the next visit starts with everything you already know.

Add a vehicle to a customer

Guard rail A malformed VIN is rejected

A VIN has 17 characters and never contains I, O or Q. A typo is caught before it reaches a recall lookup.

Each VIN maps to exactly one vehicle, which prevents a car being registered to two customers.

Step 5

Take the car in

Taking a car in opens a numbered repair ticket (such as RO-0001) and checks the vehicle onto the shop floor. From here every step is recorded on the ticket and backed up to storage such as GitHub.

Take in a car and open a repair ticket

Expand Take in a car and open a repair, choose the vehicle and describe the customer's concern in their own words. Add the odometer reading and parking bay so the car is easy to find.

How this is tested (4 steps, passing)
  1. When I take in "Sam's Camry" with the concern "Grinding noise when braking", odometer "84000" and parking bay "Bay 2"
  2. Then a repair ticket "RO-0001" is open at "Intake"
  3. And the pipeline shows 1 at "Intake"
  4. And "Sam's Camry" is on the shop floor

Guard rail A car cannot have two open repairs

One open ticket per vehicle keeps the work, parts and hours together. Finish or close the existing ticket before opening a new one.

How this is tested (3 steps, passing)
  1. Given "Sam's Camry" is taken in with the concern "Grinding noise when braking"
  2. When I take in "Sam's Camry" with the concern "Also needs an oil change"
  3. Then I am told "This vehicle already has an open repair"

The ticket is backed up

Opening the ticket backs it up with its number, so your records are safe from the first minute.

How this is tested (2 steps, passing)
  1. When I take in "Sam's Camry" with the concern "Grinding noise when braking"
  2. Then the backup history contains "RO-0001: vehicle received"

Step 7

Fix the car

Once the customer has approved, record the parts you actually use and the hours each teammate spends. These feed the invoice and the shop's profit reports.

Enter each part with its quantity and what it cost you. Part cost is tracked against the estimate so you can see your margin.

  2. Then the ticket shows "parts cost $35.00"

Track hours for each teammate

Several technicians can work on one repair. Choose the teammate and log their time; the ticket totals hours per person.

Scope: this is a local desktop shop tool backed by a local knowledge graph, with every change backed up to storage such as GitHub, not a multi-user hosted service. Payment recording notes a payment already received; it does not charge cards. Tickets are saved locally and in the shop's Git repository; backups to GitHub are set up under Repair queue, but showing them as issues in GitHub, Gitea or Jira is not built yet.
