To test how far KGraph-driven game architecture stretches into high-unit-count real-time simulation, we built Starfall Command (games/robos-rts), a deterministic real-time strategy game with a custom 2.5D angled 3D perspective renderer:

  • Three Asymmetric Factions: Command the Meridian Guard (human terran-style infantry, siege howitzer splash lines, and retractable silo gates), the Gnarl Brood (swarming bio-monsters), or the Aurelian Concord (high-tech phase-stepping walkers and cliff-striding Spire Walkers).
  • Classic RTS Economy & Controls: Full crystal and aether tap harvesting, build-grid placement validation, production queues, control groups, idle-worker alerts, supply caps, and kiting micro-management.
  • Programmatic Capture & 4-Act Demo: Exposed HTTP/IPC camera, selection, and screenshot control endpoints to drive a deterministic 4-act 1080p battle showcase.
