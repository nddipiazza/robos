How it works

Camera Watch finds ONVIF cameras with WS-Discovery and reads their stream addresses, snapshots and PTZ controls through the open-source onvif library. ffmpeg reads each camera's video, so any RTSP, RTSPS or MJPEG-over-HTTP camera works too.

Motion is detected on this computer by comparing small grey copies of each frame with a slowly-updated background. ONVIF cameras can also send their own motion alarms. Clips are copied from the camera's stream without re-encoding, so recording uses little CPU.

Video stays on this computer. Camera passwords are stored in your RobOS config folder and are never included in Config Sync backups.

Share cameras with another RobOS

Make an invite and send it to the person. Their Camera Watch joins your private network with access to these cameras only, not to your other apps or files, and you can take it back at any time.
