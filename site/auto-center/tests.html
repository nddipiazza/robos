How we know it works

The test report, recordings and the list of scenarios behind this tutorial.

Every page of this tutorial is checked by end-to-end tests. They run the real Auto Center app, click through every screen as a person would, and fail if anything is missing or wrong. The screenshots you have seen come from those runs, not from mock-ups.

115 scenarios, 115 passing. Open the full test report
