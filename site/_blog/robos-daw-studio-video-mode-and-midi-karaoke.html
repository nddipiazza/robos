Bringing multi-camera drum video and word-synced karaoke into the same Linux workstation. Earlier this week we wired a 12-mic acoustic drum kit into RobOS DAW Studio over a unified 20-channel Focusrite Scarlett 18i20 + Behringer ADA8200 ADAT clock, and earlier today we turned our LineageOS 24 Moto G100 Android phone into a 24/7 ONVIF Wi-Fi camera (packages/ip-camera-android). Putting those two pieces together unlocked the next studio workflow: mounting the phone over the drum kit as a wireless HD drum cam, viewing synchronized video in an embedded dock or second-monitor window, and right-clicking any backing song to project MIDI-mapped karaoke lyrics onto that same display.

1. Video Inputs: First-Class RobOS IP Camera (HD Default) + Optional Audio

Clicking Add Video in the topbar, edit toolbar, empty-session launchpad, or channel-rack context menu opens the upgraded Add Video Input Track dialog (packages/daw-studio/lib/video-devices.js), with RobOS IP Camera (Wi-Fi HD) selected as the primary first-class input source alongside USB / V4L2 Webcam and ONVIF / RTSP / Custom URL:

  • Triple-Path RobOS IP Camera Auto-Discovery + Remote Studio Control: discoverAllVideoSources merges saved Camera Watch devices, UDP 239.255.255.250:3702 ONVIF WS-Discovery, and a bounded 64-way concurrent /api/ping subnet sweep (~450 ms across a /24 LAN so Wi-Fi AP multicast isolation never hides your phone), then queries GET /api/status for live resolution, lens, and battery percentage. Remote buttons in both the modal and track FX Inspector post to /api/control (HttpOnvifServer.kt) to flip Back/Front lenses, toggle the LED torch, zoom, or request HD sensor presets without walking across the drum room.
  • HD Quality Video Default (DEFAULT_VIDEO_QUALITY = 'hd-1080p'): Video capture defaults to 1080p Full HD (Up to 1920x1080 · 30 fps · H.264 CRF 18), with 720p HD (CRF 19), 480p SD, and 360p Low (/sub) presets. RobOS IP Camera URLs automatically promote any saved /sub path to the HD /video main stream, while Linux V4L2 webcams negotiate compressed -input_format mjpeg at the highest supported HD resolution (1920x1080 or 1280x720 @ 30 fps instead of raw 640x480 YUYV).
  • Optional Audio Pairing & Headphone Latency Alignment: Every video track offers three audio modes—Video Only (No Audio — Default) so armed cameras record pure MP4 alongside your 20-channel Focusrite + ADAT mic tracks (or even with zero audio tracks armed), Paired Audio Interface Input (records a sample-locked .wav through the DAW console + .mp4), or Camera Stream Audio (extracts RTSP/stream audio into timeline waveform peaks). During overdubs, VideoTrackRecorder measures first-packet arrival against WebAudio headphone presentation timestamps (earsStartWallMs) to align camera startup latency automatically.

2. Video Output & Right-Click "Create Lyrics Track" (SMF Format 1 .KAR)

During playback, scrubbing, or live camera monitoring, Video Output renders your chosen visual track either inside the Embedded Video Output Dock (toggleable via Video Out or V, with compact/wide sizing) or detached into its own Standalone App Window (Pop Out -> independent Electron window with track selector, Pin Top, Fullscreen, and Dock in DAW).

Industry-standard MIDI Karaoke mapping (packages/daw-studio/lib/lyrics-generator.js). Right-clicking any music track, clip region, or timeline lane and choosing Create Lyrics Track builds a dedicated LYRICS track directly below the source audio using standard SMF Format 1 Karaoke (.kar, 480 PPQ):

  1. Binary SMF Format 1 .kar + Enhanced .lrc Sidecars: Track 0 writes the conductor map (Soft Karaoke, @KMIDI KARAOKE FILE, @T<title>, 0xFF 0x58 Time Signature, 0xFF 0x51 Tempo). Track 1 writes per-word 0xFF 0x05 MIDI Lyric Meta-Events (with standard \ / / line-break prefixes) paired with Channel 1 NoteOn/NoteOff vocal guide notes (MIDI 43..81), persisted inside <projectDir>/takes/*.kar alongside an Enhanced Word-Synced .lrc file ([mm:ss.xx] <mm:ss.xx>Word ...).
  2. 4-Stage Lyric Discovery & Vocal-Band Pitch Alignment: Cleans cover/stem filenames (e.g. Somebody That I Used To Know by Gotye Meets Metal_mixed [M4A] -> Gotye — Somebody That I Used To Know), checks local .kar/.mid/.lrc sidecars, queries open LRCLIB synced/plain lyrics, falls back to local Whisper word-timestamp ASR (Xenova/whisper-tiny.en) for custom vocals, and analyzes the clip's 300–3200 Hz vocal-band RMS envelope + normalized autocorrelation fundamental frequency (F0) to assign each word its vocal MIDI pitch note.
  3. Adaptive Karaoke Stage: When another video or camera feed is playing, lyrics render as a lower-third overlay with a 4-dot pre-phrase lead-in countdown, 60 fps gold/cyan progressive word wipe, upcoming next line, and live MIDI note badge. When no other video is playing, Video Output automatically displays the RobOS Studio Branded Dark Karaoke Stage (daw-crest emblem, dark navy studio grid, animated EQ bars, and full-stage centered karaoke lyrics).
