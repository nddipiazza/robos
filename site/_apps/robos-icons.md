RobOS SVG icon registry for built-in apps


## Icon registry

`index.js` exports `BUILTIN_APPS`, `getIcon` and `getAllIcons`. The built-in app definitions are also compiled for browser use by `build-browser.js`.

Use [Icon Manager](/icon-manager/) to browse and customize the desktop icons. When registering an app, keep the built-in app definitions and browser registry in sync.
