The in-game edition separates the supplied full-album recording into its twelve
tracks and adjusts their levels and transition edges for background playback.

The title screen plays the full-album masters. The gameplay/ tracks form a
separate gameplay edit in album order. The edit uses selected passages with
the estimated vocal stem removed from the vocal tracks. Hidden Agenda
(Instrumental) keeps its original arrangement. Gameplay tracks crossfade into
one another over six seconds.
