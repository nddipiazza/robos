RobOS Auto ontology
Schema.org provides the upstream vocabulary. RobOS extends it for parts fitment, service, ownership and shop workflows. Listed schema types describe the domain; only garage/service entry and reference imports are implemented here.

Sources requiring separate access
VIN decoding is public. Title/brand/odometer/salvage history comes through NMVTIS-approved providers. Previous-owner identities and plate records are not an open bulk dataset. OEM repair manuals, labor times and parts fitment usually require separate licensed sources. Owner-provided records can be maintained locally.
