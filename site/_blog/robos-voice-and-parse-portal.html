To make the desktop environment and Knowledge Graph accessible by voice and capable of indexing massive enterprise document stores, we shipped three major subsystems in late September:

  • RobOS Voice (packages/voice-prompt): Bi-directional voice assistant combining natural Kokoro-82M / Edge-TTS neural speech with offline Whisper STT, Rhasspy/whisper_streaming adaptive energy VAD, suffix-prefix utterance stitching, PipeWire live waveform preview, and real-time voice execution of RobOS skills.
  • KGraph Parse Portal & Luxir C++ Search (packages/kgraph-parse-portal): Built the ingestion gateway fronting Apache Tika 4.0 gRPC connectors, contextual MIME disambiguation, and the built-in Luxir C++ search index (alongside Solr, Elasticsearch, and OpenSearch schemas).
  • The Gig Bandit (getemgigs.com) & E-Learning Tracks: Shipped the production Next.js live-music discovery app and nextjs-vercel-web App Wizard template, plus interactive RobOS eLearning courses and role-based training tracks for Non-Developers, Systems Architects, Agent Harness Engineers, and Senior Reviewers.
