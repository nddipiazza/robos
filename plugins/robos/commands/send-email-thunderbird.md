# Send Email through Thunderbird

Send an email with the user's Thunderbird account (Gmail from RobOS Cloud Accounts) through the RobOS Thunderbird Bridge add-on and Thunderbird's MailExtension compose API. Confirm recipients, subject and body with the user before sending.

## Input

$ARGUMENTS — Recipients, subject, body or what to write, optional cc/bcc, attachments and From address

## Procedure

Follow instructions in `plugins/robos/skills/send-email-thunderbird/SKILL.md`.
