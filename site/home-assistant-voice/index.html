Home Assistant Voice (NC-VK-9727) Two-Way Intercom

RobOS Home Speaker features first-class support for the Nabu Casa Home Assistant Voice Preview Edition (NC-VK-9727). Speak announcements out loud with Kokoro neural TTS, ask a question from your workstation or coding agent, and have the speaker automatically open its dual-microphone array to record and transcribe your spoken answer locally with Whisper—without needing a wake word, button press, or cloud server.

2. Two-Way Voice Architecture

RobOS Home Speaker (packages/home-speaker, debug port :19200) talks directly to the Home Assistant Voice Preview Edition (NC-VK-9727) over your local Wi-Fi network on TCP port :6053 using the native ESPHome protobuf wire protocol (packages/home-speaker/lib/esphome-voice-service.js).

3. Incoming from Speaker (Listen Mode) & Hands-Free Auto-Recording

  • Hands-Free Question & Auto-Listen (start_conversation = true): By setting field 4 (start_conversation = true) on VoiceAssistantAnnounceRequest (msgType 119), the speaker plays the question and automatically transitions into microphone capture the moment playback ends—no wake word and no button press needed.
  • 550 ms Acoustic Echo Guard: When the microphone opens immediately after TTS playback, EspHomeVoiceService discards the first 550 ms of room reverberation so the speaker never mistakes its own tail echo for your voice.
  • Record from Speaker Now: Click Record from Speaker Now in the UI to play a brief chime on the speaker and open its microphone immediately from your desk.
  • Wake Word & Center Button: In background Listen Mode, you can also say "Okay Nabu" or press the center button on top of the speaker at any time to send a voice note to your computer.

4. Add Smart Speaker Wizard

  1. Open RobOS Home Speaker and switch to the Speakers & Setup tab (or click + Add Speaker in the top bar).
  2. Select Home Assistant Voice Speaker (#1 in the catalog). Technical specifications stay tucked neatly inside collapsible ▸ Show technical details sections.
  3. If your speaker is brand new, expand the Bluetooth Wi-Fi setup section, click Scan Bluetooth, enter your 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi credentials, and click Send Wi-Fi to Speaker.
  4. Test the 12-LED RGB light ring, 48 kHz FLAC chime, and Kokoro neural greeting before saving.
