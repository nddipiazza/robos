Hardware rack: Behringer Ultragain Digital ADA8200 (MIDAS ADAT) + Focusrite Scarlett 18i20 (3rd Gen) on a single ALSA clock. Miking a full acoustic metal drum kit takes more than 8 preamps. Our rack pairs a Behringer Ultragain Digital ADA8200 (8 MIDAS mic preamps feeding ADAT Optical Toslink into Ch 9–16 for Kick In, Snare Top, and the Rack Toms) with a Focusrite Scarlett 18i20 (3rd Gen) (Ch 1–8 analog inputs for Hi-Hat, Floor Toms, and 3 Drum Room mics). Under PipeWire's default UCM split profile, Analog and Direct2 ADAT appear as separate virtual sources; recording them in separate processes introduces startup offset and phase comb-filtering between close mics and room mics. DAW Studio (robos-lib/audio-devices.js) unifies all 20 channels onto a single sample-locked hw:USB,0 capture clock, exposes all 437 Scarlett kernel mixer controls (Air, Pad, Line/Inst, +48V), and aligns overdubs to headphone presentation timestamps with sub-millisecond accuracy.

Why our acoustic kick sounded too low at first — and fixing it with spectral measurement & dual-band mid-punch DSP. When we first dialed in Kick In against dense metal guitars, individual 16th-note kick strokes blurred into low rumble. Measuring the recorded Kick In WAVs band-by-band revealed why: an un-triggered felt beater inside an acoustic shell puts 22–26 dB more energy at 60–110 Hz (-35 dB) than at 900 Hz – 2.2 kHz (-58 to -64 dB), even though the microphone's hit-to-bleed SNR across 800 Hz – 3 kHz was a clean +20 to +24 dB. Boosting a 72 Hz low shelf only drove sub-bass harder into the broadband VCA compressor, ducking the midrange further! Meanwhile, aggressive waveshaper saturation on room mics amplified 0 dBFS cymbal fizz, so we disabled waveshaper exciters by default (3367f766) and re-engineered the console insert chain (buildTrackWebAudioChain, 5da853ac):

  • Sub-Boom Cleanup Pre-Compressor: 65 Hz 24 dB/oct cascaded Butterworth HPF + -8.5 dB @ 115 Hz low shelf + -7.0 dB @ 275 Hz (Q = 1.9) box-mud cut removes low-end bloat before the signal reaches the VCA detector.
  • Dual-Band Articulate Mid-Punch + Smooth High Cut: Added a dedicated chest-knock peaking stage (+14.0 dB @ 1.0 kHz, Q = 0.82) paired with a live-sweepable Mid Punch Inspector fader (+15.5 dB @ 1.95 kHz, Q = 0.92) and a 4.6 kHz Butterworth LPF + high-shelf cut so every bass-drum note punches cleanly through rhythm guitars with zero brittle high-end click.
  • 6 ms Live Hot-Swap, Click-to-Seek & Transport Startup Lock: Dragging faders or EQ ramps live AudioParam nodes in place (`8–12 ms`), Solo/Mute/Trigger toggles crossfade in 6 ms (`d2b7d8c3`), clicking anywhere on waveforms or the ruler seeks live without stopping (`5778f262`), and clicking Play synchronously locks isStartingPlayback and disables #btn-transport-play while decoding large WAVs so pressing Space early can never spawn a second overlapping audio channel (`beccdd9d`).
  • Full-Width 1920px Layout, Skinny 24px Telemetry Footer & From -> To Range Mixdowns: Removed the redundant 192px left rail (`98483670`), anchored save state + SELECTION LENGTH + TRACK LENGTH + SESSION LENGTH in a non-shifting 24px bottom grey status bar (`1fa17222`), added Viterbi multi-tempo click-track segmentation (`4499aa98`), and built the Export Audio Mixdown modal supporting ruler-drag / right-click From -> To ranges across MP3, WAV 24-bit, FLAC, M4A, OGG, Opus, and AIFF (`97dbf0ab`).
