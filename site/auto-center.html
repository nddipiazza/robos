RobOS Auto · Maintenance center

A repair shop, one step at a time

Auto Center gives each person in your shop their own menu: the front desk takes the call, the mechanic does the work, checkout collects the money, and the owner sets it all up. Every change is backed up, so you never lose a customer’s history.

Quick start: your first repair in 10 minutes

Open Auto Center and switch Workspace to Maintenance center. You will see four menus under Shop desk.

  1. Admin: add yourself and your mechanics under Team. Optionally import your supplier’s parts list under Parts catalog.
  2. Front desk: press Start an intake and follow the five steps. You finish with a numbered ticket, such as RO-0001.
  3. Front desk: open the ticket from Estimates to prepare, itemize parts and labor, then record the customer’s approval.
  4. Mechanic bench: pick your name, open the ticket and press Take this ticket. Request parts, log your time and press Finish the job.
  5. Checkout: pick the car, issue the invoice, record payment and hand back the keys.

Tip: everything you type has suggestions, such as makes, models, trouble codes, parts and jobs. You can always type something that isn’t in the list.

Owner: Set up the shop (owner admin)

The Admin menu is for the shop owner. Add and manage the team, keep the parts catalog, review the questions the front desk asks about every problem, set the shop's details and connect backups.
