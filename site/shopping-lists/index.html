2. Form-Driven Item Entry, Tactile Buttons & Smart Multi-Store Paste

Typing raw bullet points on a tiny phone keyboard while pushing a shopping cart is frustrating. Every screen in RobOS Shopping Lists — on desktop and on mobile — uses touch-friendly form controls and one-tap action buttons:

  • Structured Add Item Card: Enter the item name, tap − or + to set quantity, choose a unit (ea, pack, lb, oz, gal, case, bag, box, bunch, bottle, jar), let detectAisle() auto-assign the store aisle (or pick one manually), add an optional estimated price ($) and brand/coupon note, and pick a highlight badge (🔥 Must Have, 🏷️ Sale / Coupon, 🌿 Organic, ⭐ Store Brand / Fav).
  • Duplicate Merge & 1-Tap Staples: Clicking any ＋ Staple chip or adding an item that is already unchecked on the list automatically increments its quantity instead of creating cluttered duplicate rows.
  • Per-Row Action Buttons: Every item row features a large circular check-off button (with celebratory confetti when you finish a list), inline − and + quantity steppers, an Edit modal button, and a 🗑️ Remove button backed by a 12-step ↩ Undo stack and 🧹 Clear Checked.
  • Smart Multi-Store Paste (100% Offline + Optional AI): Paste a comma- or newline-separated string such as 2 gal organic milk $4.89 (whole), 4 avocados, laundry pods @target, kirkland paper towels @costco and RobOS automatically parses quantities, units, prices, notes, badges, and store tags — routing each item to Grocery, Target, or Costco in one click.

3. Viewable in Google Docs & Google Drive on Mobile + Interactive Mobile Form

You asked for shopping lists that are viewable in Google Docs / Google Drive on a mobile device and easy to add/remove items in a form-like manner with buttons. RobOS delivers both together:

  1. Native Google Docs in Google Drive (application/vnd.google-apps.document): Clicking ☁️ Sync All 3 Lists to Google Docs & Drive uses your RobOS Google Cloud Account (lib/google-drive-sync.js) to create or update a RobOS Household Shopping Lists folder in Google Drive containing three formatted Google Docs plus shopping-lists.json. You can share the folder with a partner or family member right from the sidebar.
  2. Two-Way Checkbox & Line Pull from Google Docs: If you check off an item (changing ☐ to ☑ or [x]) or type a new line (☐ Swiffer WetJet Refill Pads — 2 box) inside the Google Docs mobile app, parseGoogleDocText() (lib/google-docs-format.js) pulls and merges those mobile edits back into RobOS on sync.
  3. Live Mobile Web Form (:19225) & Serverless Google Drive Apps Script: On home Wi-Fi or RobOS Private Networks, scanning the built-in QR code opens the live mobile form on port 19225 with real-time Server-Sent Events (SSE). For 5G cellular inside the store when your home PC is asleep, RobOS also uploads Mobile-Form-Apps-Script/Code.gs and Index.html to your Drive folder so you can deploy a serverless Google Apps Script Web App backed directly by your Google Drive files.

Open http://127.0.0.1:19225/guide in any browser and click 🖨️ Print Guide (or run node packages/shopping-lists/bin/shopping-lists.js guide) for a clean cheat sheet you can pin to the fridge:

Command / SyntaxExampleEffect
AI Skill / Slash Command/shopping-lists list [grocery|target|costco|all]Shows items to buy, items in cart, aisle groupings, and estimated totals.
Cross-Store Add/shopping-lists add "2 gal milk, tide pods @target, rotisserie chicken @costco"Routes each comma-separated item to Grocery, Target, or Costco with auto-detected aisles.

Voice ("Hey Nabu")"What is on my Costco list?" · "Add 2 gallons of milk to grocery"Speaks remaining items & estimated total aloud or adds items hands-free via RobOS Voice.
