We rewrote our long-term architectural thesis into The Skill Commons whitepaper and plain-English summary at rowbose.com/future: instead of asking frontier data centers to regenerate solved routines from scratch for every app, software tasks are decomposed into content-addressed, verified WebAssembly (WASI 0.2 / WIT) leaf skills and snapped together locally by a small model on your own machine.

  • rowbose.com v2 & 31-Slide Live Hero: Promoted the new flat, light-by-default front site to rowbose.com (archiving classic docs under /v1/), featuring a 31-slide interactive player and full video recording that traces a real task from planning to merged PR.
  • Small-Business Playbook & Contributor Internships: Added the "Make Money with RobOS" small-business section on the homepage, replaced placeholder app letters with native RobOS SVGs across /apps/, and framed open-source contributions at /contribute/ as a public context-engineering internship.
