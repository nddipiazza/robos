The **Front desk** menu is where every repair starts. It walks you through five steps, so you never have to remember what to ask. You can use it while the customer is on the phone.

::shot[prepare-the-estimate-and-record-approval-from-the-front-desk|1|The Front desk home screen: start an intake, log a conversation, and see what needs attention]

Press **Start an intake** and follow the steps along the top: **Contact, Customer, Vehicle, Issue, Confirm**.

## Step 1: How did they reach you?

Choose **Phone call**, **Text message**, **Email**, **Walk-in**, **Website** or **Other**. It is saved on the ticket, so anyone can see how the customer got in touch and reply the same way.

::shot[a-new-customer-phones-in|2|Choosing how the customer reached the shop]

## Step 2: Who are they?

Choose **Existing customer** or **New customer**.

### An existing customer

Type any part of their **phone number, email or name**. Matches appear after three letters, and you pick one.

::shot[an-existing-customer-is-found-by-phone-number|4|Typing a phone number finds the customer and shows their cars]

If nobody matches, a yellow note offers **Add as a new customer** and carries over what you typed.

::shot[an-unknown-customer-can-be-added-straight-from-the-search|5|A search that finds nobody offers to add a new customer]

### A new customer

Enter their **name** and a **phone number**, and an email if you have one. Choose how they like to be contacted, so the next person replies the way the customer wants. If you only have an email, that is enough.

::shot[a-customer-texts-in-and-prefers-text-messages|4|A new customer who prefers text messages]

> **Safety net.** A new customer needs a phone number or an email. Without a way to reach them you cannot send the estimate, so the wizard asks for one.

If your customer is also in RobOS People, open **Link to RobOS user or group** to connect them. This is useful for fleet or business accounts.

## Step 3: Which vehicle?

For an existing customer, their saved cars appear as big buttons. Pick the one they are calling about, or press **Another vehicle**. Cars are shown everywhere as make, model and year followed by the customer, such as *Toyota Camry 2015 — Sam Rivera*. A nickname is optional and appears in brackets.

For a new car, type year, make and model. As you type, suggestions appear: try typing `2015 honda` in **Find year, make and model** and choose a result.

::shot[a-new-customer-phones-in|5|Finding a vehicle by typing year, make and model]

::shot[add-another-vehicle-for-an-existing-customer|7|Adding a second car for a customer who is already on file]

> **Safety nets.** The year, make and model are required, so you always know exactly which car you are looking at. A car that already has an open ticket is greyed out and shows its ticket number, so nothing is opened twice.

::shot[a-car-that-already-has-an-open-ticket-cannot-be-taken-in-twice|6|A car that is already on a ticket cannot be taken in again]

## Step 4: What is wrong?

Pick the closest kind of problem: brakes, engine, noise, leak, electrical, heating and air conditioning, steering and tires, transmission, routine service or something else. The wizard then asks questions for that kind of problem, such as *what does it sound like* and *when does it happen*, followed by a few details it asks every time: warning lights, when it started, whether it is safe to drive, how urgent it is and whether the customer will wait.

::shot[a-new-customer-phones-in|9|Answering the brake questions with one tap each]

Type the customer's own words in the box at the bottom. Their phrasing often helps the mechanic.

For routine service, choose every service they want, such as an oil change and a tire rotation.

::shot[routine-service-takes-several-choices|10|Choosing several routine services at once]

> **Urgent cars.** If the customer says the car is unsafe, or must be towed, the ticket is marked **Urgent** and appears first for the mechanics.

If the car must be towed and you run your own trucks, the confirm step asks **Send our tow truck?** Say yes and enter where the car is: the tow call opens with the ticket. See [Run a tow service](../10-tow-desk/).

## Step 5: Check and open the ticket

You see a summary of everything you entered. Add the odometer reading and where the car is parked if you know, then press **Open ticket**.

::shot[a-new-customer-phones-in|11|The summary before the ticket is opened]

You get a numbered ticket such as **RO-0001**, and the car is checked in on the shop floor. From the finish screen you can start another intake or go straight to the estimate.

## Prepare the estimate

Back on the Front desk home screen, **Estimates to prepare** lists tickets that need one. Open the ticket, add each part and each labor line with its price, and press **Send estimate**. As you type, your parts, common parts and standard jobs are suggested, and your labor rate is filled in for you. The total is worked out for you.

::shot[prepare-the-estimate-and-record-approval-from-the-front-desk|3|Itemizing parts and labor on the estimate]

Contact the customer however they prefer, then record their answer on the same screen. Choose how they replied (in person, phone, email or text message) and press **Customer approves** or **Customer declines**. A declined estimate goes back to diagnosis, so you can send a revised one. Only the latest estimate can be approved.

::shot[prepare-the-estimate-and-record-approval-from-the-front-desk|5|The approval is recorded in the ticket history]

> **Important.** Auto Center records the conversation, but it does not send the text or email itself. Send the estimate your usual way, then record what the customer said.

## Log every call, text and email

Press **Log a call, text or email** on the home screen, pick the ticket, choose the channel and who contacted whom, and write what was said. It is saved on the ticket for everyone.

::shot[log-a-call-text-or-email-about-a-ticket|3|A conversation logged on a ticket]

## Watch the follow-ups

The home screen also lists **Waiting for the customer to approve**, so you know who to call back, and **Mechanic recommends more work** for extra work the mechanic found.

## You are ready when

- [ ] You have taken one test call all the way to an open ticket
- [ ] You have sent an estimate and recorded an approval
- [ ] You have logged one conversation on a ticket
