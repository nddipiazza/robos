<h1>Run a repair shop from first call to paid and returned</h1>
<p class="lead">Auto Center follows every customer car through intake, diagnosis, estimate, approval, repair, invoice, payment and offboarding. Each repair is a DevOps work item, committed to your shop's private Git history. This guide is built from the same end-to-end tests that prove it works: 31 scenarios, 31 passing, every screenshot taken from the real app.</p>

<section class="guide-chapter" id="team"><p class="eyebrow">Step 1</p><h2>Build your shop team</h2><p class="chapter-intro">Before a car comes in, set up the people who will work on it. Teammates can be linked to a person in RobOS People so the shop uses the same identities as the rest of RobOS, and each teammate's hours are tracked per repair.</p>

<section class="guide-chapter" id="customers"><p class="eyebrow">Step 2</p><h2>Add a customer</h2><p class="chapter-intro">Customers are the people who bring cars in.
